Fps Settings and their uses:

Low 20fps

1- Can glitch into walls many ways, Standard is jumping with a gun with your back against the wall,
then shooting when you touch the floor and pressing x at the same time to glitch

Low 30fps

1- High selfboost (Jump then press x and slice up)

Low 60fps 

1- Short selfboost (Jump then press x and slice up) (only used to jump out of acid)
3- Can survive acid better even touching small acids without dying or suffering damage (acid walk)

high 30fps:

1- Slicing up and moving right or left makes you move very far before falling

high 60fps:

1- Can fly by moving mouse left and right on torso and holding x
2- Can slide on walls by clicking/slicing to the opposite side of the wall
3- Can survive acid better even touching small acids without dying or suffering damage (acid walk)
4- Slicing moves you the opposite way you slice, you can drop with x and slice down to move a lot
5- Has bad selfboost
