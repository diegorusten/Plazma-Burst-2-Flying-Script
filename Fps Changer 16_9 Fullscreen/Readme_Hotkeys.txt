These are the hotkeys to use the script and start flying and changing FPS inside the game

F1 = Start Script
F2 = Stop Script

Shift+F = toggle high 60 / low 30
Shift+R = toggle low 20 / low 30
Shift+E = change to high 30
Shift+Q = change to low 60

R_click = show FPS
ALT + R_CLICK = toggle shooting or slicing with right click


How to change fps fast:

Open the script, on fullscreen game, press F1 then Shift+F twice to set the fps
