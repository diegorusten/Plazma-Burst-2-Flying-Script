# PB2 Flying Script (AutoHotkey v2)

This Script works with 2560×1440, 1920x1080 and 1920x1200

press  **CTRL+SHIFT+B** to hide the bookmarks and use it with taskbar ON

# Hotkeys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **F1** | Start script |
| **F2** | Stop script |
| **Shift + F** | Toggle High 60 FPS / Low 30 FPS |
| **Right Click (High 60 FPS)** | Start flying |
| **Right Click (Not High 60 FPS)** | Show current FPS and physics |
| **Alt + Right Click** | Toggle right click passthrough (shooting/slicing) |
| **Shift + Mouse Wheel** | Increase/decrease flying movement range |
| **Shift + R** | Toggle Low 20 FPS / Low 30 FPS |
| **Shift + E** | Change to High 30 FPS |
| **Shift + Q** | Change to Low 60 FPS |

---

# How to Fly Fast

1. Open the script.
2. Start the game in fullscreen mode.
3. Press: F1
4. Press: Shift+F
5. Hold: R_Click and X

# FPS Settings and Uses

Different FPS settings provide different movement mechanics and abilities.

## Low 20 FPS

1. **Wall Glitches**
   - Can be used to glitch into walls in different ways.
   - Standard method:
     - Jump with a gun while your back is against a wall.
     - Shoot and press **X**  when you touch the floor.

## Low 30 FPS

1. **High Selfboost**
   - Jump, then press **X**, then slice upward to perform a strong selfboost.


## Low 60 FPS

Uses:

1. **Short Selfboost**
   - Jump, press **X**, then slice upward to perform a smaller boost.
   - Only used when jumping out of acid

2. **Acid Walking**
   - Allows better survival when touching small acid areas.
   - Can reduce damage from brief acid contact.

## High 30 FPS

1. **Long Slicing Movement**
   - Slicing upward while moving left or right allows you to travel a large distance before falling.

## High 60 FPS

Uses:

1. **Flying**
   - Move the mouse left and right around the character's torso while holding **X** to fly.

2. **Wall Sliding**
   - Click/slice toward the opposite side of a wall to slide along it.

3. **Acid Walking**
   - Allows better survival when touching small acid areas.
   - No damage walking on low acid.

4. **Reverse Slice Movement**
   - Slicing moves you in the opposite direction of the slice.
   - Example:
     - Drop using **X**.
     - Slice downward to gain a large movement boost.

5. **Bad selfboost**
   - High 60 FPS have a bad selfboost.
